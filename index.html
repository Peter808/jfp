Jose Felix Perez is a Miami muralist and painter. He started on walls with the graffiti crew Guerilla Tactics, earned his BFA at New World School of the Arts in 2010, and that same year painted a five-story, 60-foot mural for Greenberg Traurig. His walls run from Wynwood to Detroit. His canvases stay closer to home, in the light and overgrowth of South Florida.

He has painted with Michael Vasquez in Wynwood and at Detroit's Murals in the Market, and he has run production on public art projects by Assume Vivid Astro Focus and Boa Mistura in Miami Beach. His 2018 solo show, The Magic Hour at Swampspace, was reviewed by Hyperallergic.
